## 📋 STORY IDEA TEMPLATE - EXAMPLE

### Story Overview
**Title**: "The Midnight Garden"

**Genre**: Primary: Gothic Romance, Secondary: Victorian Historical with Supernatural elements

**Setting**: 
- **Time Period**: 1887 Victorian England
- **Location**: Ravenshollow Estate in the Yorkshire Moors - a sprawling manor with an infamous midnight garden where flowers only bloom under moonlight
- **Atmosphere**: Haunting and romantic, with mist-covered moors, ancient stone corridors, and a garden that seems to exist between worlds

**Logline**: When botanist Evelyn Thorne arrives at the mysterious Ravenshollow Estate to catalog its legendary midnight garden, she discovers that the garden's magic is tied to a centuries-old curse—and the brooding lord of the manor may be both her salvation and her doom.

### Main Characters

#### Protagonist
- **Name**: Evelyn Thorne
- **Age**: 26
- **Background**: Brilliant botanist and daughter of a deceased professor, she's made a name for herself studying rare flora despite Victorian society's limitations on women. Recently lost her father and inherited his debts.
- **Personality**: Intelligent, curious, stubborn, compassionate but guarded due to past heartbreak
- **Goal**: To complete her father's research on mystical plants and secure her financial independence
- **Flaw**: Her scientific skepticism blinds her to supernatural truths, and she struggles to trust others after betrayal

#### Love Interest
- **Name**: Lord Adrian Blackthorne
- **Age**: 32
- **Background**: The current Lord of Ravenshollow, heir to a cursed bloodline. His family has guarded the midnight garden for generations, paying a terrible price.
- **Personality**: Brooding, protective, intelligent, carries deep guilt and responsibility
- **Dark Secret**: He's bound to the garden's curse—every full moon, he transforms into a creature of shadow and must feed the garden with his own life force. He's slowly dying.
- **Connection to Plot**: The garden's curse is tied to his family's betrayal of a witch centuries ago. Only true love's sacrifice can break it.

#### Antagonist
- **Name**: Lady Cordelia Ashford
- **Motivation**: She's Adrian's cousin who wants to inherit Ravenshollow. She's discovered the truth about the curse and plans to ensure Adrian dies so she can claim the estate and destroy the garden to build a modern estate.
- **Methods**: Manipulation, sabotage of Evelyn's work, attempts to accelerate Adrian's curse
- **Connection**: She was Adrian's former fiancée who broke their engagement when she learned about the curse, but now wants the estate's wealth

#### Supporting Cast
- **Mrs. Grayson**: The loyal but secretive housekeeper who knows the family's dark history
- **Thomas Fletcher**: The head gardener who helps maintain the midnight garden and serves as a mentor figure
- **Dr. Jonathan Mills**: Evelyn's colleague and potential rival love interest from London
- **The Ghost of Lady Morgana**: The original witch whose curse created the midnight garden, appears to guide Evelyn
- **Jasper**: Adrian's faithful but mysterious manservant who may be more than he appears

### Plot Structure

#### Three-Act Breakdown
**Act 1 - Setup (Chapters 1-5)**:
- Inciting incident: Evelyn receives a mysterious letter offering employment at Ravenshollow to study the midnight garden, with payment enough to clear her debts
- World establishment: Evelyn arrives during a storm, discovers the manor's gothic atmosphere and the garden that only blooms at night
- Character introductions: She meets the brooding Lord Adrian, who seems reluctant to have her there but needs her expertise
- Initial mystery: Plants in the garden seem to respond to emotions, she witnesses strange transformations during her first full moon

**Act 2 - Development (Chapters 6-10)**:
- Rising tension: Evelyn begins documenting impossible botanical phenomena, grows closer to Adrian despite his attempts to keep distance
- Relationship development: Stolen moments in the moonlit garden, Adrian protects her from increasingly dangerous supernatural events
- Major obstacles: Lady Cordelia arrives and reveals she was Adrian's former fiancée, Dr. Mills comes to "rescue" Evelyn from the "backward estate"
- Midpoint twist: Evelyn discovers Adrian's transformation during a full moon and learns about the family curse

**Act 3 - Resolution (Chapters 11-15)**:
- Crisis point: Cordelia sabotages Evelyn's research and tries to poison Adrian to accelerate the curse's effects
- Final confrontation: Evelyn must choose between her scientific skepticism and accepting the supernatural to save Adrian
- Resolution: She performs a ritual in the midnight garden, offering herself as a willing sacrifice to break the curse
- New equilibrium: The curse is broken but transformed—the garden becomes a bridge between worlds, and Evelyn and Adrian become its guardians

#### Key Mysteries/Conflicts
1. What makes the midnight garden's impossible botany function, and why do the plants seem sentient?
2. Can the growing attraction between Evelyn and Adrian overcome the supernatural barriers keeping them apart?
3. What is the true nature of Adrian's curse, and can Evelyn's scientific mind accept magical solutions?
4. Who is working against them, and why do they want the garden destroyed?

### Themes & Atmosphere

#### Primary Themes
- "Science and magic can coexist when approached with an open heart"
- "Love requires sacrifice, but true sacrifice is freely given"
- "The past's sins can be redeemed through understanding and forgiveness"

#### Gothic Elements
- A sentient garden that exists partially in another realm
- Adrian's monthly transformation as part of an ancient family curse
- The ghost of Lady Morgana who appears during crucial moments
- Ancient stone circles and mystical symbols throughout the estate
- Flowers that bloom with memories and can show visions of the past

#### Atmosphere Descriptors
- Readers should feel: Enchanted yet unsettled, romantically charged, mystical wonder
- Dominant imagery: Moonlight through ancient windows, flowers glowing in darkness, mist rolling across the moors, candlelight in stone corridors
- Important sensory details: The scent of midnight jasmine, the sound of wind through stone passages, the touch of petals that feel almost like skin

### Style Preferences
- **Pacing**: Slow burn romance with gradually accelerating supernatural reveals
- **Heat Level**: Steamy romantic tension with passion that builds through emotional connection
- **Violence Level**: Moderate - supernatural danger and threats but not graphic violence
- **Supernatural Level**: Obvious magic but with scientific curiosity - Evelyn approaches magic like she would any natural phenomenon
- **Ending Type**: Happy with transformation - curse broken but they choose to remain guardians of the garden

### Inspiration & References
- **Books**: "The Ten Thousand Doors of January" by Alix E. Harrow, "The Invisible Life of Addie LaRue" by V.E. Schwab
- **Visual**: Kew Gardens at night, Gothic Revival architecture, Pre-Raphaelite paintings of garden scenes
- **Themes**: Beauty and the Beast, Persephone and Hades, Secret Garden with supernatural elements

### Special Requests
- Include detailed botanical descriptions of impossible plants (flowers that sing, vines that respond to emotions, trees that grow memories as fruit)
- Show Evelyn's scientific mind trying to rationalize magical phenomena through detailed observation and note-taking
- Create romantic tension through shared wonder at the garden's beauty rather than just physical attraction
- Include a scene where Evelyn and Adrian dance in the midnight garden surrounded by glowing flowers
- The final ritual should combine scientific methodology with magical practices
